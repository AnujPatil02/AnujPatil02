## Featured Projects

### 1. NeuroPlant AI — Manufacturing Intelligence & Industrial IoT
An Industry 4.0 platform for a simulated automotive manufacturing facility. Integrates machine telemetry through MQTT and OPC-UA with machine monitoring, OEE analytics, predictive maintenance, quality and energy insights, and a 3D digital twin.

**Focus:** Industrial IoT, smart manufacturing, predictive maintenance and operational intelligence.

### 2. WindSense AI — Wind Turbine Predictive Maintenance
A multi-agent predictive-maintenance project for a simulated wind farm. Combines sensor anomaly detection, remaining useful life estimation, energy forecasting and maintenance decision support.

**Focus:** Applied ML, time-series forecasting, IIoT and renewable energy analytics.

### 3. HydroSight — Reservoir Intelligence & Hydropower Optimisation
A reservoir analytics and decision-support platform covering water-level and hydropower forecasting, uncertainty estimation, anomaly detection and water-release optimisation across Indian reservoirs.

**Focus:** Data science, forecasting, optimisation and environmental analytics.

### 4. OpsIntel AI — Manufacturing Operations Analytics
A manufacturing analytics project using simulated production data to examine OEE, downtime, quality, energy and plant performance through SQL analysis and BI dashboards.

**Focus:** SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python and business intelligence.
